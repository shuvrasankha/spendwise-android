Delete your SpendWise account

This page is for the SpendWise app (developer: SpendWise). Deleting your account is permanent. It cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SpendWise and sign in.
  2. Go to More, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm, and sign in again when asked.

Without the app

Email contact.spend.wise.app@gmail.com from the email address you use for SpendWise, with the subject "Delete my SpendWise account". We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your expenses, income, debts you created, investments, budgets, goals and settings.
  • Your sign-in (the account itself).

What is not deleted

  • A debt another person created and shared with you stays in their account, because it is theirs.
  • Anonymous crash reports kept by Firebase Crashlytics, which contain no money data and are not linked to your records.

See also the privacy policy.
